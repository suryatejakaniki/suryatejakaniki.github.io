Privacy

This site has two parts. The pages you read here (the home page, the articles and this note) set no cookies and load nothing from other sites. The labs need a free account, and this note explains what that involves.

What is collected when you sign in

  • From the sign-in method you choose (Google, GitHub or a link sent to your email): an account ID, your email address and your display name. Sign-in is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication. This site never sees your password.
  • On the lab server: when you joined, when you last used a lab, whether you asked to hear about new labs, and what you did in each lab: opening it, completing steps, using hints, showing reference queries, checking the timeline and the assessment. Each of these is recorded once, the first time. For the daily limit, the server also counts how many queries you ran each day and how many result rows and characters they showed. Your queries are run, but their text is not stored.
  • In your browser: your progress (queries, pinned rows and answers) is saved in your browser's storage so you can pick up where you left off. It stays on your device, and it stays after you sign out unless you tick Also clear my progress on this device when you sign out. On a shared computer, tick it.

Why

To give subscribers access to the labs, to keep your place, to see how the labs are used so they can be improved, and, only if you tick the box, to email you when a new lab is published.

Where it is kept

  • Google Firebase Authentication keeps the sign-in accounts.
  • Cloudflare runs the lab server and its database, which holds your subscriber record, lab activity and daily counts.

Your details are not sold or shared with anyone else. There are no ads and no tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Turn lab announcements on or off at any time under Account in the lab.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove saved progress.

Fair use

Each account can run up to 400 queries a day. The daily row counts show when an account reads far more data than a lab needs; accounts that misuse the labs, for example by copying their data in bulk, can be blocked.

Questions about this note can be sent through the GitHub profile linked on the home page.

All lab data is synthetic: reserved example domains, documentation IP ranges and fictional phone numbers. People and organizations in the labs are invented.
